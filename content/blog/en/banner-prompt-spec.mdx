## How to use it

1. Paste the whole spec into ChatGPT, Gemini or any AI image generator of your choice.
2. Answer the interview, or don't. It asks one question at a time and names a recommended pick for each. You can go through them one by one, answer in your own words, say "your pick" for a single question, or say "choose the rest for me" and let it decide everything that is left.
3. Check the summary it gives you. It lists every locked option plus the exact prop the figure will hold before anything gets drawn. If something needs changing, fix it here instead of waiting until after it generates. This is especially useful if you are using a free plan.
4. Confirm your choices, and it generates your banner.

Your copy is the only text that appears in the image, so write the headline before you start. Nine words maximum. If you are using it on LinkedIn, I recommend leaving your name and job title out of the headline because they are already on your profile.

## The options

| Category           | Options                                                                         |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Platform           | X header 1500×500, LinkedIn cover 1584×396, or custom                           |
| Layout             | L1 text left / art right, L2 art left / text right, L3 text only, L4 image only |
| Art family         | M marble sculpture, P painted storybook                                         |
| Art direction      | A1–A6 within marble, B1–B6 within painted                                       |
| Subject effect     | E0 clean cutout, E1 particle, E2 halftone, E3 engraving, E4 blueprint           |
| Palette            | P1 rust, P2 purple, P3 cobalt, P4 forest                                        |
| Background texture | F1 flat, F2 paper grain, F3 grainy gradient                                     |
| Typography         | T1 neo-grotesk, T2 soft geometric                                               |

## Author's note

One thing you should know before you use it.

This prompt comes with a fixed set of design variants. If a lot of people use it as-is, their banners will start to look alike.

So treat it as a starting point for your own banner, and as a source of inspiration.

It gives you a working system. From there, you can change any part to fit your taste and make the result your own.

> If you are happy with the result as it is, that is completely fine. You do not have to change anything.

But if you want a design that feels like your own instead of everyone else's, here is the process I followed.

1. **Go to Pinterest first and look at art.** I searched for marble sculpture art and for painted classical art, and I saved the ones I liked. Then I searched for effects, like halftone dots or particle dissolve, to see how other designers use them.

   One important thing here. Use those images as a reference only, never as something to copy. The images on Pinterest belong to the people who made them, and many of them are not free to use. So look at them to understand the style you want, describe that style in your own words, and let the AI generate something new for you.

2. **Write down what you see.** When you find an art style you like, describe it in the same shape as the directions already in the prompt: what the figure is doing, how close the camera is, what the surface looks like, what the light does. Then add it to the list as a new option.

3. **Change the colors.** The four palettes are only four lines of hex values. Every other rule in the prompt talks about color by its role (field, ink, secondary, accent), not by its name, so you can replace the hex codes and nothing else breaks.
